# AERIX / A new point of view.

First website iteration. Sam authorized complete-drone verification, web optimization and the cinematic landing page without an approval pause. The four finished interior layers and approved exterior are the design source. Camera optics use the existing fitted assembly in this iteration.

- Build: full premium, real Three.js model, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, locally vendored dependencies, no build step.
- Content: the established AERIX identity, with new editorial copy about observable design; no invented flight time, range, resolution, certification, pricing or availability.
- Mood: precise, sculptural, quiet, exploratory.
- Palette: near-black carbon #101212, warm ivory #efeee7, copper #c5a180; muted gray #a0a5a1.
- Type: Instrument Sans for large, tightly set display and readable body; IBM Plex Mono for chapter indices and annotations.
- Motion: expo.out, 1.15-second weighted wheel scroll, native touch momentum, 0.9-second reveals. One GSAP ticker drives Lenis and rendering. No automatic spinning.
- Signatures: a continuous scroll-controlled product disassembly/reassembly; a restrained chapter index with spatial labels tied to the model.
- Journey: assembled hero → form and finish → open architecture → battery → flight computer → camera optics → reassemble and fold → explore the design.
- Interaction: clear chapter links, pause motion preference, functional fullscreen design explorer with orbit and assembly controls, replay. No nonfunctional purchase or signup buttons.
- Performance: remove historical GLB payload, preserve animated pivots, compress geometry and textures, reduce repeated draw calls, defer detail assets where useful. Measure actual bytes and browser frame timings.
- Access: readable semantic sections, skip link, keyboard controls and focus states; reduced-motion/static fallback, graceful loading failure, 390 px layout. The page remains readable before the model is ready.
- Deliver: local site, optimized runtime assets, complete-drone verification and optimization reports, desktop/mobile captures, first-iteration notes and tuning controls.
